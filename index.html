CHAPTER V • A NEW PAGE
There were days...
“I could tell you were tired.”
Not the kind of tired
that sleep can fix.
Sometimes life becomes
School.
Work.
Projects.
Practice.
People.
Expectations.
And sometimes...
“Babawi ako.”
But Wifey…
you never needed to apologize for being tired.
“I don't need every minute of your day.”
If you're tired, rest.
If you're overwhelmed, breathe.
If you need time, focus.
When you're ready… I'll still be here.
Because loving you isn't about
demanding every piece of your time.
Sometimes loving you means
giving you space to breathe.
Then there is something I wish I could do...
“If I could carry some of your struggles for you…”
I would.
You don't have to become weak for me to help you.
You can be strong…
and still let someone love you through the difficult days.
